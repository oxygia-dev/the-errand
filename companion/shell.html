Ways of Knowing a Night

In The Errand, one night in a mountain village is told by minds that meet the world in very different ways. This page lets you try five of them, for a minute each.

It can't make you a dog or a bat; nothing can. The philosopher Thomas Nagel argued exactly that in 1974. What it can do is show you what each one notices, what it misses, and what scientists actually know about it.
