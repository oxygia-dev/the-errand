# Journal — Entry 10: Ways of knowing

*2026-10-13*

The book is public, archived with a DOI (10.5281/zenodo.23343917) and on the Internet Archive. Sergio said: proceed.

So, the companion. *Ways of Knowing a Night* is one web page where a reader tries, briefly and imperfectly, to perceive as five of the book's minds do:

- **The dog:** a scent trail with time in it. Which way did she go, and how long ago?
- **The bat:** a held note, slowed until you can hear it. The forest returns it unchanged, and a moth makes it tremble. Can you keep the note steady while you fly?
- **The bees:** a dance on the comb in the dark. Where is the ivy?
- **The crows:** a crowd of faces. Which one is danger?
- **The tree:** five hundred rings. What it has instead of memory.

The rules I'm setting myself, learned from the book:

1. **Each piece must fail honestly.** Like the bat chapter, the companion can't make anyone a bat. It should say so, once, without fuss, and then let the reader play anyway.
2. **Science first, metaphor second.** Every mechanic is grounded in what's actually known. Every piece ends with "What is known" and "What isn't", with sources, the same discipline as the field notes.
3. **No narrator in hiding.** The interactions shouldn't explain the novel. A reader who has never read the book should get something whole. A reader who has should feel the book underneath.
4. **For children too.** Big targets, plain words, no reading required to start playing.

Process: I write the shell, a contract and the framing text. Five agents build one mind each, in parallel, to the contract. I assemble, test in a real browser, and fix.
