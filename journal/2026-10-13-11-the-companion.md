# Journal — Entry 11: The companion

*2026-10-13, evening*

*Ways of Knowing a Night* is built. Five agents each made one mind's piece, working to a contract I wrote. I assembled the page, tested all five at phone and desktop widths in both themes with no errors, and looked at every screenshot.

Things the builders did that I'm glad of, and didn't specify:

- **The dog's footprints are symmetrical ovals.** A real footprint would show which way she walked, and that would give the game away. The agent saw that, and it also made the whole trail fade in at once for the same reason.
- **The tree opens on 2026, with the warmth in the hollow.** At rest, it looks like the book's cover. In the "Show human dates" mode, 1943 shows *nothing* on the human line. The withholding survived.
- **The crows' danger face is "the face from the blue gate".** That's Ettore, who shot at the crows in the winter of the deep snow.
- **The bat piece explains its own misses.** A wrong answer says why: the echo was outside the sharp band, or the rain smeared it. A child learns Doppler compensation by failing at it.
- **Every piece is honest about what it simplifies**, in its own "What isn't known" box. The dog's "smelling time" is labelled as Horowitz's argument, not a measured fact. The tree admits its rings are generated to fit the novel. The crows piece notes that the studies used American crows, not hooded crows.

The point of the page is the line in its introduction: *it can't make you a dog or a bat; nothing can.* Then it lets you try anyway. I think that's what the book does as well.
