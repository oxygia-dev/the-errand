## 6. The search — 20:21 to 01:10

*Operational log, advanced command post (PCA), Pietrafonda. Compiled from radio traffic, phone notes and the Carabinieri station diary. Times are local.*

---

**20:21** — NUE 112 call received from Bar Centrale, Pietrafonda (caller: Lucia C., proprietor). Reports minor female missing. Transferred to Carabinieri, Compagnia di Castel di Sangro.

**20:24** — Details. Missing person: **IDA M.**, 8 yrs, resident Pietrafonda (via del Forno 6) since March. Height approx 1.30 m, slim, dark hair cut short. Clothing per mother: blue jeans, blue padded jacket, **red wool hat**, white trainers, purple school rucksack with planet design. Mother: Chiara M., 38, translator, works from home. Father resident in Rome, informed by mother.

**20:24** — Last seen: *uncertain*. Mother last saw child approx 13:30. Child habitually spends afternoons at the house of an elderly neighbour (via del Forno 4). Time child left neighbour's house: not known.

**20:26** — Patrol dispatched from Castel di Sangro (Brig. Lalli, App. Sc. Marini). ETA 35 min.

**20:30** — Note (Lucia C., by phone): residents searching village on foot. Cemetery checked, negative. Two residents (**Franco D.**, 61, and **Sandro P.**, 44) have gone up the *mulattiera* (mule track) behind the cemetery toward the Rio Freddo ford with torches, on basis that a village dog that "follows the child" was seen returning wet from that direction at approx 18:50. Advised by phone to proceed with caution and keep phone on.

**20:44** — Weather: Protezione Civile Abruzzo yellow alert, thunderstorms, upper Sangro valley, 20:00–02:00. Radar shows cell approaching from W.

**21:02** — Patrol on scene. Brig. Lalli takes statement from mother at Bar Centrale.

**21:06** — Mother's statement (summary): last saw child at lunch. Child had attended the San Michele procession that morning with a neighbour (Giuseppina R., 74). Mother went at 19:45 to collect child from elderly neighbour's house. **Neighbour stated child had "gone home" with bread.** Child not at home; rucksack not at home. No known reason for child to leave. No problems at home or at school. Child "afraid of the dark". No phone. No medical conditions.

**21:10** — Brig. Lalli at via del Forno 4. Elderly neighbour: **Assunta D.**, 90, lives alone. Per neighbours, affected by dementia; a care monitoring device is installed (daughter's contact on file: Rosa D., Pescara).

**21:14** — Brig. Lalli, note of conversation with Assunta D., verbatim as far as possible:

> Q: Signora, did you see the little girl this afternoon? Ida?
> A: Yes. She was here.
> Q: Do you know what time she left?
> A: Before dark. I told her, before dark.
> Q: Do you know where she went?
> A: *L'ho mandata io.* I sent her. Up at the Romito. With the bread. For my mother.
> Q: Your mother, signora?
> A: She's waiting. With the little one. They have nothing up there. *(Pause.)* You have to go before the Germans come.
> Q: *(Repeated question re: time.)*
> A: You're a good boy. Are you Concetta's?

Note (Lalli): *Signora calm, oriented to person, not to time. Statement regarding destination "Romito" made spontaneously and repeated. Romito = cave/hermitage approx 1h15 on foot above village via mulattiera; destination of this morning's procession, which child attended. Considered of low reliability given evident confusion (references to "Germans", deceased mother) but cannot be excluded. Note consistent with direction of travel suggested by dog (see 20:30). Reported to Compagnia.*

**21:16** — Compagnia acknowledges. Lead logged: *child may have gone toward Romito. Reliability: LOW. Corroborating: MOD (dog direction, procession route familiar to child).* Residents Franco D. and Sandro P. already en route on that axis.

**21:17** — Phone contact, Franco D.: reached ford at approx 21:00. Water low, normal. Crossed. No sign of child. Calling continuously, no response. Bear tracks seen on far bank, fresh — "she-bear with the little ones, the one who comes for the plums". Continuing up toward Romito.

**21:20** — Mother asks to join residents on the track. Advised to remain at home address in case child returns. Mother complies.

**21:26** — Prefettura dell'Aquila activates **provincial plan for the search of missing persons**. Requests: Vigili del Fuoco (VVF) Castel di Sangro + mountain/SAF team; CNSAS (Soccorso Alpino) Stazione Alto Sangro; Carabinieri Forestali; canine unit (trailing dog) from L'Aquila; air support.

**21:31** — Phone contact, Franco D.: approx 400 m above ford, in forest. Heavy thunder overhead. Lightning visible through trees. Calling child by name. No response. "Nothing. Only the trees. If she's up here, she can hear us. She'd answer."

**21:34** — Compagnia orders residents to descend immediately: lightning risk in forest, no equipment, no radio. Residents protest. Order repeated.

**21:38** — Air support: **helicopter not available**: storm cell over area, ceiling below ridge, lightning. Reassess at 23:00.

**21:40** — **Storm on Pietrafonda.** Heavy rain, hail briefly, strong W wind. Two power interruptions in village (21:41, 21:47), restored.

**21:43** — Giuseppina R. (neighbour) asks Lalli whether someone should inform Assunta D.'s daughter "because the signora is agitated and I can't stay with her all night". Lalli: yes, inform her.

**22:07** — Franco D. and Sandro P. back at cemetery. Report: **ford now knee-deep and rising fast, brown water, stones moving.** Crossed with difficulty holding a rope from Sandro P.'s car. Sandro P. lost his footing; minor injuries to hand. Neither reached Romito. Turned back approx 600 m below it.

**22:12** — Note (Lalli): Franco D. states, unprompted: "If she was up there she'd have heard us. We called the whole way. A child hears her name, she shouts." Recorded.

**22:15** — VVF Castel di Sangro on scene, 6 operators + SAF fluvial team requested from L'Aquila. CNSAS Stazione Alto Sangro on scene, 8 operators. Carabinieri Forestali, 2. **PCA established in piazza San Michele** (church porch, out of rain). Coordinator: VVF duty officer.

**22:20** — Initial briefing. Search sectors defined on 1:25,000 map:
- **Sector V** — village, houses, outbuildings, stables, vehicles, wells.
- **Sector R** — Rio Freddo: ford to stone bridge (1.5 km) and bridge downstream to confluence (3 km).
- **Sector M** — mulattiera, cemetery to ford.
- **Sector F** — forest above ford, incl. Romito. *Not accessible on foot from village while ford in spate.* Alternative access: forestry road from Passo della Croce (approx 50 min by 4x4 from village, then 400 m on foot). Road condition after storm unknown; Forestali report two landslips on it in 2025.

**22:24** — Sector V: hasty search assigned to residents + 2 VVF. Sector M: CNSAS team 1. Sector R: VVF.

**22:31** — Mother's phone: call from child's father, Rome. Father intends to drive to Pietrafonda. Advised of weather.

**22:50** — **VVF, Sector R: red wool hat found** in branches of a willow overhanging the Rio Freddo approx 30 m below the stone bridge, on the true right bank, at water level. Wet through. Recovered.

**22:58** — **Hat identified by mother as the child's.**

**23:00** — Coordinator, reassessment. Hat confirms child's presence on or near the Rio Freddo upstream of the bridge. Most probable scenarios:
- **(A)** Child reached the water at the ford or elsewhere upstream and entered the water (fall, attempted crossing), carried downstream. Hat consistent.
- **(B)** Child lost hat at the water (wind, stumble) but did not enter, and remained on near or far bank.
- **(C)** Child crossed before the spate and is in Sector F.

Note: residents Franco D. and Sandro P. passed through Sector M and the lower part of Sector F between approx 21:00 and 22:00 calling continuously with no response. Weighs against (C) and against a child conscious and within earshot in lower Sector F.

**23:02** — **Priority to Sector R.** VVF SAF fluvial team ETA 23:30. All available personnel to both banks of Rio Freddo, ford to confluence. Light towers requested.

**23:05** — Air support reassessment: negative. Storm cell persisting.

**23:08** — Mother informed of priority to river by Brig. Lalli and VVF officer. Mother asks to go to bridge. Accompanied by Lucia C.

**23:12** — Note (Lalli, in station diary): *Have passed the elderly neighbour's statement re: the Romito again to PCA coordinator. Coordinator notes it is on file, consistent with Scenario C, and will be addressed via forestry road at first light or when road confirmed passable. Coordinator considers Scenario A most urgent given hat and spate: "if she is in the water we have minutes, if she is in the forest we have hours."*

**23:40** — Canine unit arrives from L'Aquila: handler + trailing dog (*cane molecolare*, female bloodhound, 6 yrs). Scent article: child's pillowcase, collected by handler from bed, bagged.

**23:52** — Dog starts at child's home address. Rain easing to moderate.

**23:58** — Dog trails from home address **to door of no. 4** (elderly neighbour's house) and indicates. Then from no. 4, **up via del Forno, NOT toward home**, to cemetery road. Handler notes trail strong and directional despite rain: "a fresh-ish trail, this evening's, going up."

**00:07** — Dog passes cemetery gate without entering, continues onto mulattiera. Handler notes possible second, older trail of same subject on same route (morning procession, per mother). Dog follows the stronger.

**00:16** — Mulattiera, approx 200 m below ford. Dog working well. Handler notes **trail contaminated** by passage of residents at 21:00 and 22:00 and by CNSAS team 1 at 22:30. Dog persists.

**00:31** — **Dog at ford.** Dog trails to water's edge at the stepping stones and **indicates at the water.** Will not continue. Casts along near bank 50 m up and down, returns to the stones, indicates again. Torrent in spate, approx 1.2 m above normal, impassable. Handler's assessment: *"Trail ends at water. Child reached the water at this point. Cannot determine from this side whether she crossed."*

**00:34** — CNSAS team 1 by torch from near bank: **bear tracks visible in mud on far bank**, large and small. Forestali confirm: adult female with two cubs of the year known to be frequenting orchards on village edge; sightings at dawn on 5 of the last 7 days.

**00:37** — Forestali advise: Marsican bear, adult female with cubs. No recorded attacks on humans in the park. However, female with cubs is the configuration of greatest caution. Recommends no personnel in Sector F on foot at night.

**00:40** — Note: CNSAS team 1 leader to PCA: *"Hat below the bridge, dog stops at the water, tracks on the other side. Either she's in the river or she's over there with the bear. I know which one I'm more afraid of. Neither of them helps us tonight."*

**00:45** — Coordinator, plan for night:
- Sector R: continue, maximum resources. VVF SAF fluvial team active from 23:35 on both banks; second team requested from Pescara.
- Sector F: **at first light**, CNSAS + Forestali via forestry road from Passo della Croce to Romito and upper forest, conditional on road. Thermal drone (VVF) to fly Sector R as soon as rain permits, then Sector F.
- Sector M/V: complete.

Probability of area (POA), initial, by consensus at PCA:
- **R** (river corridor): **0.50**
- **F** (forest above ford, incl. Romito): **0.25**
- **M + V** (track, village, other): **0.15**
- **Outside all sectors** (vehicle, other): **0.10**

**00:52** — Brig. Lalli asks that the elderly neighbour's statement be recorded in the POA rationale for Sector F. Recorded: *"Sector F supported by: dog direction (20:30), trailing dog to ford (00:31), neighbour statement re: Romito (21:14, low reliability). Weighed against: no response to calls by residents (21:00–22:00); bear presence; child's reported fear of dark."*

**00:58** — Rain easing further. Thunder now E of ridge.

**01:04** — Mother at stone bridge with Lucia C. and a VVF operator. Declines to return to house. Has been given a thermal blanket.

**01:10** — Note (Lalli, station diary, personal): *Mother asked me whether the river is cold. I said I didn't know. It is.*
