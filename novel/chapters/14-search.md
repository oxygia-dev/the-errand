## 14. The search — 02:10 to 06:55

*Operational log, PCA Pietrafonda, continued.*

---

**02:10–02:50** — VVF thermal drone (SAPR), Sector R, ford to confluence. Rain stopped. Returns: 2 × VVF fluvial operators (known), 1 × fox, 3 × small mammal. **No human signature in or beside the water.**

**02:55** — Sector R ground search continuing. Fluvial team 2 (Pescara) on scene 02:40. Water level at bridge falling: −0.3 m from peak.

**03:01** — Call to Brig. Lalli's mobile from **Rosa D.** (daughter of Assunta D.), now at via del Forno 4.

**03:09** — Brig. Lalli to PCA coordinator, in person. Summary of call:
- Assunta D., on waking, stated again that she gave the child bread and sent her to the Romito "before dark".
- **Half a loaf of bread is missing from Assunta D.'s bread chest.** Remaining half present, torn not cut. Daughter states her mother does not eat this bread. Not in bin, not on table.
- Daughter states her mother told her (approx 1976) of sheltering overnight as a child in winter 1943 in **a hollow beech** above the ford on the Romito path, with the instruction to shelter in it "if you can't see the path". Daughter believes her mother may have told the child the same.
- At 03:05 Assunta D., addressing her daughter as her mother, said where she had sheltered: *"after the water... where it turns... the sheep... I went left... down... the big one, the one they cut."* Rosa D.'s interpretation: first hairpin above the ford, at the boulder known locally as *la pecora*; left of path (ascending); downhill; a large old pollard beech.

**03:12** — Coordinator: the missing bread is **physical corroboration** of the 21:14 statement. Timing consistent with dog (18:50 return) and trailing dog (indication at ford). Hat in river consistent with loss at ford, not necessarily entry into water. Bear tracks on far bank do not exclude a child having passed before.

**03:14** — Note: resident Franco D. recalled to PCA and asked about his route at 21:00–22:00. Confirms he passed the "sheep rock" (*"la pecora, sì, la conoscono tutti"*) going up and coming down, calling. Did not leave the path. Did not look at trees. "Why would we look at trees? We were calling her. She'd have answered."

**03:16** — **POA revised:**
- **F** (forest above ford, incl. Romito + tree): **0.60**
- **R** (river corridor): **0.20**
- **M + V**: **0.10**
- **Outside**: **0.10**

**03:18** — Revised plan. **Team F1** (CNSAS ×4, Forestali ×1, trailing dog + handler) and **Team F2** (CNSAS ×3, VVF ×2, medical kit, vacuum stretcher) to depart by 4×4 via forestry road from Passo della Croce. Objective 1: Romito (confirm presence, look for sign). Objective 2: descend mulattiera from Romito to ford, locate "sheep rock" and candidate trees. Sector R search to continue at reduced strength.

**03:22** — Forestali: forestry road partially obstructed at km 4.2 (landslip, 2025), passable with care. Expect 50–60 min to road end, then 400 m on foot to Romito.

**03:40** — Brig. Lalli informs mother at stone bridge. Mother requests to join teams. Request declined (vehicle capacity, terrain, night). Mother advised to wait at cemetery gate at foot of mulattiera. Mother complies, accompanied by Lucia C.

**03:56** — Teams F1, F2 depart PCA.

**04:21** — Air support: VVF helicopter (Drago, Pescara) available from first light, approx 06:30, weather permitting. Low cloud on ridge.

**04:30** — Mother's phone: child's father, delayed at Avezzano (A24 closed, landslip). Informed of new hypothesis.

**04:52** — F1/F2 at end of forestry road. Track heavy, debris. Proceeding on foot with head torches. Calling child by name at intervals.

**05:00–05:35** — VVF thermal drone, Sector F (upper), launched from Passo della Croce. Cloud base low. **Canopy closed** (beech, full leaf; first yellowing only). Returns through gaps: roe deer ×3; wild boar ×1; **large mammal + 2 small** moving upslope near cliff base approx 1,550 m (Forestali: consistent with bear, female with cubs; moving away from path); Teams F1/F2 (known). **No signature consistent with a child.** Drone operator notes: *"Under this canopy we only see what's in the gaps. If she's under a tree we won't see her. If she's inside one, nothing will."*

**05:09** — **F1 at Romito.** Cave mouth and hermit's cell searched. No child. **Found: on stone shelf beneath iron cross, among candle stubs from yesterday's procession, a half loaf of white bread wrapped in a tea towel.** Dry (sheltered). End crust missing (torn). Bread placed upright, *"set out carefully, like something on an altar"* (F1 leader). Photo sent to PCA 05:11.

**05:12** — Photo shown to Rosa D. by Brig. Lalli (by phone message). Rosa D. confirms the tea towel is her mother's (embroidered initials A.D.).

**05:13** — **Child's presence at Romito confirmed.**

**05:14** — Also found at Romito: on the dust of the stone floor, small shoe prints (trainer sole), several, partly overlaid by procession prints; candle-wax drip on floor below shelf with a small fingerprint in it. Bat droppings, fresh. No other sign.

**05:16** — Trailing dog started at Romito shelf (scent article: pillowcase). Dog takes a trail out of the cave and **down the mulattiera**, slowly. Handler: trail very weak after rain; dog working from patches under rock overhang and dense litter. *"She's going down. That's all I can tell you."*

**05:31** — F1, approx 200 m below Romito on mulattiera. Trailing dog loses trail on open wet scree. Casts, does not recover. Handler: *"Rain's taken it."* Teams continue down the path on foot, searching both sides, calling.

**05:40** — F2: hollow beech approx 30 m left of path (descending), below a rock outcrop. Checked: **negative** (dormouse nest, old).

**05:48** — F1: second hollow beech, large, approx 60 m right of path (descending). Checked with torch: **negative.**

**05:53** — F1 leader to PCA: *"There are hollow beeches all over this slope. Half the old ones are hollow. And there are rocks everywhere and half of them look like sheep. We need the hairpin. We need the first hairpin above the ford, coming up. Coming down, that's the last. We're looking for the last hairpin."*

**06:02** — Ford: water level falling fast; approx 0.4 m above normal. Sector R team 3 at near bank. Assess crossable with rope by approx 06:30.

**06:11** — F1/F2 descending. Visibility improving (nautical twilight). Calling continuously. **No response.**

**06:18** — Note (F2 leader): *"If she's here, she can hear us. We're loud. Either she's not here, or she's not able to answer, or she's choosing not to."* Coordinator requests medical readiness for hypothermia, possible injury.

**06:22** — Brig. Lalli, by phone to F2 leader, passes on a point raised by Rosa D.: her mother's stories of the war include an instruction *to stay still and silent if you hear voices, "even if it's someone you know."* Daughter is uncertain whether her mother repeated this to the child. F2 leader acknowledges.

**06:31** — Ford: Sector R team 3 crosses with rope. Two operators on far bank. Bear tracks of last evening still visible, partially washed. Start up mulattiera toward F teams.

**06:36** — **Unaccompanied dog** observed by Sector R team 3 crossing the ford upstream of the rope, swimming the last metre, climbing the far bank: large, white, grey ears. Identified by Sandro P. (by phone) as the village dog ("Biscotto", "nobody's"). Dog goes up the mulattiera ahead of team 3 at a trot, nose down. Team 3 does not attempt to stop it.

**06:40** — F1 at a hairpin with a large grey moss-topped boulder. F1 leader: *"Could be. Could be any of three."* Calling. No response. Team spreads out across slope left of path (descending: right) to check trees.

**06:47** — Sunrise in 18 minutes. Light under canopy still poor.

**06:52** — Sector R team 3 by radio: *"The white dog's left the path. Just above us, at a big rock. He's gone off to the left, downhill, into the trees. Fast."*

**06:53** — F1 leader: *"Which rock?"*

**06:55** — Sector R team 3: *"The one like a sheep."*
