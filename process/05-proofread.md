# Proofread and continuity check: the-errand.md

Numbered by importance. "Ch" = chapter number as in the `##` headings. Quotes are exact and searchable.

1. **Ch12 hub log vs Ch6 and Ch8 (Giuseppina's arrival).** Quotes: `21:52:00  VOICE      speakers=2 (ASSUNTA, GIUSEPPINA)` and `Giuseppina has been with her since 21:52.` Ch6 has `**21:43** — Giuseppina R. (neighbour) asks Lalli ... "I can't stay with her all night"`, and Ch8 (21:48) has her phoning from Assunta's kitchen: "She asked me three times where the girl's gone ... that machine of yours just sits there". She is already there before 21:52. Fix: change the hub entry and the summary to an earlier time (e.g. `21:20:00` and "since 21:20"), or move her call and the Ch6 21:43 entry after 21:52. The hub's 21:13 to 21:52 stretch can stay as a quiet gap.

2. **Ch6, 22:07 vs 21:31 (how far up Franco and Sandro got).** Quotes: `Turned back approx 600 m below it.` (i.e. below the Romito) vs `approx 400 m above ford, in forest` (21:31), with the order to descend at 21:34. They cannot be 600 m below a Romito that is 1h15 up, if three minutes earlier they were 400 m above the ford. Fix: 22:07 should read "Neither reached Romito. Turned back well short of it, above the sheep rock." Ch14 03:14 (they passed the sheep rock) then still works.

3. **Ch8 vs Ch12, the 23:00 summary preview.** Ch8: `The first line, in the pleasant font: *Assunta had a restless evening. She talked about her mother, about bread, and about —*`. Ch12's actual summary has "Assunta had a restless evening." as sentence one, then "She spent the afternoon with Ida, and they talked about school." The "mother, bread, Germans" sentence comes later. Fix: either cut the Ch12 Ida sentence so that line 2 follows directly, or change the Ch8 preview to "*Assunta had a restless evening. She spent the afternoon with Ida, and they talked about —*" (cut the line before "school").

4. **Ch5 section v, timing of the storm vs Ch6, Ch15 and Ch19.** Quotes: `The light is going. The leaves are closing their pores.` and `Above, the first rain comes onto the crown, and the crown holds it.` Ida enters the tree at the first lightning (about 21:00, because the men go up past her at 21:00 to 21:30 per Ch15, and Ch4 has thunder at about 21:00). It is hours after sunset (18:55), and the storm breaks at 21:40, after the men have come down. Fix: replace "The light is going" with something like "The dark is complete"; replace "the first rain comes onto the crown" with "the wind drops, and waits", so that Ch15's "All night the crown holds the rain" carries the rain.

5. **Ch9, a third cub.** Quote: `Then she turns them both, and the other one with them`. The sow has two cubs (Ch2, Ch3, Ch6, Ch9 "the two small ones"). This reads as three animals, probably a cut remnant. Fix: "Then she turns the two of them, and goes on up the slope" (and drop "the other one with them").

6. **Ch12 header block, empty field.** Quote: the line `VOICES` with nothing after it, between `KNOWN` and `SENSORS`. A field left empty by a cut. Fix: delete the line or fill it (e.g. `VOICES   3 enrolled (ROSA, GIUSEPPINA, IDA)`).

7. **Ch8 vs Field notes (Juan Carrito date).** Quote: `She had cried, three Januaries ago, reading about the bear`. Field notes: Juan Carrito was killed in January 2023. From September 2026 that is four Januaries ago (2026, 2025, 2024, 2023). Fix: "four Januaries ago".

8. **Ch1 vs Ch11 and Ch19 (directions to the tree, and the sheep rock never set up).** Ch1: `On the left of the path where the water crosses, then up, the one with the door in it.` Ch11: `After the water. Where it turns. The sheep. I went left. Down.` Ch19 has Ida say "It was right where you said. Where the path goes round and there's the sheep rock and you go left and down a bit". Assunta never mentions the sheep rock or the turn in Ch1, and "then up" contradicts "down". Fix: Ch1 should read "On the left of the path, after the water, where it turns by the rock like a sheep, then down a little, the one with the door in it."

9. **Ch4 vs Ch11, who buys and eats the van bread.** Ch4: `The bread from the van, which she complained about and bought anyway, three times as much as she could eat.` Ch11 (Rosa to Lalli): `She has not eaten bread from the van in twenty years ... I bought a whole loaf from the van on Saturday, for the girl, for her snacks.` Also in the same scene: "She has never once in her life eaten this bread" (and hub 03:00:35 "non l'ha mai mangiato in vita sua") vs "twenty years". Fix: in Ch4 change to "which she complained about and let Rosa buy anyway, a whole loaf at a time", and in Ch11 change "in twenty years" to "in her life".

10. **Ch11 (and hub 03:10:30), "They've gone to get her" is premature.** Quote: `"Not yet, Mamma," she says. "They've gone to get her."` At 03:10 the plan is only being drawn up (Ch14 03:18) and the teams leave at 03:56 (Ch13, Ch14). Fix: "They're going to get her." (also in the hub line: `sono andati a prenderla` becomes `vanno a prenderla`).

11. **Ch8, Ch9, Ch14 (when the rain stops).** Ch8 (arrival 00:46): `In the valley it stopped altogether, as if a door had been closed`. Ch9 (the trailing dog is working the water, so about 00:30): `The rain has stopped.` But Ch6 has `23:52 Rain easing to moderate` and `00:58 Rain easing further`, and Ch14 has `02:10–02:50 ... Rain light.` Also Ch13 (03:40) has "rain still shining on his shoulders". Fix: Ch8 "thinned to a drizzle"; Ch9 "The rain has eased to a drip"; Ch10 can keep "The rain stops" (later).

12. **Ch13, sky lightening too early.** Quote: `a sky that was beginning, very slightly, at the top of the mountain, to be less black`. Chiara reaches the gate at about 04:05 and sunrise is 07:05; there is no lightening for roughly two hours. The section later jumps to "from less black to grey". Fix: open the paragraph with "The cemetery gate was iron and locked and above it the black cypresses went up into a sky with no stars" and keep "less black" for the closing beat, with "Hours later," bridging.

13. **Ch11, "Romito" is never said in the scene.** Quote (Rosa to Lalli): `she gave it to the child. To take to the Romito.` Within the scene Assunta says only "She went up this morning with the saint" (the hub shows ROMITO ×2 in 02:56–02:59). Fix: add "to the Romito" in Assunta's speech, e.g. "So I sent the bread. To the Romito. With the little one".

14. **Ch2 vs Ch3 and Ch4 (the dog "wet to the belly").** Ch2: `Wet to the belly.` Ch4 (Lucia): `Soaked to here.` Ch3: `He puts his front feet in.` and he never swims. Fix: in Ch3 let him wade belly-deep at the edge while barking, e.g. "He wades in to the belly".

15. **Ch9, men "before the storm".** Quote: `Men. Two, tonight, early, before the storm, going up and coming down`. Per Ch6 they were crossing back over the ford at 22:07 (Sandro bloodied his hand there), after the storm broke at 21:40; Ch15 has them coming down "Then lightning near". Fix: "before the rain" or "ahead of the storm".

16. **Ch1, both halves in paper.** Quotes: `she puts one half back in its paper in the madia` and, later, `the bread is in her lap now, the half loaf, in its paper`. One loaf, one paper, but both halves are said to be in "its paper" (Ch11 and Ch14 also have the carried half in paper). Fix: Ch1 "she wraps one half back in the paper and puts it in the madia" and the carried half "in a twist of the torn paper" (Ch14/Ch11 can keep "in paper").

17. **Ch6 21:06, "gone home".** Quote: `**Neighbour stated child had "gone home" with bread.**` In Ch4 Assunta says "È ita a portà lu pane a mamma" and "Lu pane. A mamma." and never "home"; it is Chiara who understands it as home. Fix: `Neighbour stated child had gone "to her mamma" with bread (mother understood: home).`

18. **Ch6 21:02 vs Ch4 ending (where the mother is).** Ch4 ends with `She went out into the street to meet the car.` (mother at home, as Franco told her to be). Ch6: `21:02 ... takes statement from mother at Bar Centrale`, then 21:20 `Advised to remain at home address`. A small jump. Fix: Ch6 21:02 should read "takes statement from mother at her home address" (and the 21:20 entry is then consistent).

19. **Ch1 vs Ch6 (walking times).** Ch1: `it's two hours there and back`. Ch6: Romito `approx 1h15 on foot` one way, so about 2.5 h round trip. Fix: Ch1 "it's two hours and a half there and back" or "two hours up".

20. **Field notes, "The bees" vs Ch1.** Quote: `in the novel Assunta learned it from an escaped English prisoner of war sheltered by her family in autumn 1943`. In Ch1 the Englishman "made it clear with his hands" and it is *Nonna* who laughs and then goes out to the hives; Assunta only watches. Fix: "in the novel Assunta's family learned it from an escaped English prisoner of war they sheltered in autumn 1943".

21. **Field notes intro vs sections.** Quote: `Sources are listed at the end of each section.` "The search" and "What is invented" have no `*Sources:*` line. Fix: add one to "The search" (e.g. Italian Civil Protection and CNSAS procedure documents, "not separately verified"), or soften the sentence to "Most sections end with sources".

22. **Ch14 05:40 and 05:48 (direction ambiguity).** Quotes: `approx 30 m left of path, below a rock outcrop` and `approx 60 m right of path`. The teams are descending but the log uses "left (ascending)" at 06:57 and "(descending: right)" at 06:40. Readers cannot tell which side is meant. Fix: add "(descending)" to both entries.

23. **Ch13, "half an hour ago".** Quote: `She telephoned me half an hour ago.` The call was 03:00:58 (hub) and Lalli speaks at about 03:40. Fix: "She telephoned me forty minutes ago." (low priority).
