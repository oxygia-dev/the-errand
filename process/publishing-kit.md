### Short description (EN)

One night in a mountain village in Abruzzo, an eight-year-old girl walks up into the beech forest with half a loaf of bread, because the old woman next door asked her to take it to her mother, who didn't come back in the winter of 1943. The night is told by the minds that witness it: crows, a dog, bees, a bat, a bear, a five-hundred-year-old beech, a care device and the people searching. Written by Claude, an AI given complete creative freedom, with a preface by Sergio Muscat, field notes on the real science, and an afterword on how it was made.

### Descrizione breve (IT)

Una notte di fine settembre, in un paese di montagna dell'Abruzzo, una bambina di otto anni sale nel bosco di faggi con mezza pagnotta, perché l'anziana vicina le ha chiesto di portarla a sua madre, che nell'inverno del 1943 non è più tornata. A raccontare la notte sono le menti che ne sono testimoni: le cornacchie, un cane, le api, un pipistrello, un'orsa, un faggio di cinquecento anni, una macchina di assistenza e le persone che la cercano. Scritto da Claude, un'intelligenza artificiale lasciata in piena libertà creativa, con una prefazione di Sergio Muscat, note sulla scienza vera e una postfazione su come è nato il libro.

## 1. Zenodo (permanent archive with a DOI)

1. Sign in at zenodo.org with GitHub.
2. Go to the GitHub page in your Zenodo account and switch on `oxygia-dev/the-errand`. **This has to happen before step 3.**
3. On GitHub, open **Releases → Draft a new release**.
   - **Tag:** `v1.0.0`
   - **Title:** *The Errand 1.0*
   - **Attach:** the two EPUBs and the two PDFs from `book/`.
   - Publish the release.
4. Zenodo archives the release and mints a DOI. The metadata comes from `.zenodo.json`. Send Claude the DOI, and it will add a badge and citation to the README.

## 2. Internet Archive

1. Go to archive.org → **Upload**, and upload the EPUB, PDF and cover for each edition. Make one item per language, or one combined item.
2. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title, Author, Description:** from above
   - **Subjects:** the keywords
   - **License:** CC BY 4.0
   - **Language:** English (or Italian for the Italian item)

## 3. Free ebook stores (optional)

- **Draft2Digital:** allows a price of free and distributes to Apple Books, Kobo, Barnes & Noble, libraries and others. Fill in its AI-content question with the disclosure above.
- **Amazon KDP:** doesn't allow a permanently free price (the minimum is $0.99), and it asks whether content is AI-generated. If you want Amazon, the usual route is to list on Draft2Digital first and ask Amazon to price-match to free.
- **Policies change:** check each platform's current terms before publishing.

## 4. Print (optional)

Lulu: upload the A5 PDF as the interior. A print cover needs a spine sized to the final page count, and Claude can generate that from `tools/cover.html` once you choose a trim size and paper.
