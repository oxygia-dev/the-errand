# Candidate reviewers in Abruzzo for "La commissione"

Purpose: find a native Abruzzese reader or institution to check dialect, local customs, village-life plausibility, the San Michele procession, WWII (Gustav Line) history, bear and forest details, and mountain-search procedure.

Compiled 2026-10-12. Nobody has been contacted.

## Read this first: how reliable the contact details are

## A. Dialect, customs, village life, the procession

### 1. Prof. Francesco Avolio, Università dell'Aquila (Dipartimento di Scienze Umane)
- **Fit:** Full professor of Italian linguistics (since Oct 2023, per his CV). He has taught "Dialettologia e geolinguistica" and published on Abruzzese and Aquilano dialects, the ALEICA linguistic and ethnographic atlas, and the ethnolinguistics of transhumance. He is the best match for both the dialect lines and the pastoral and mountain vocabulary.
- **Route:** Use the official profile or contact page on scienzeumane.univaq.it. No email was shown in the search results.
- **Sources:**
  - CV: https://scienzeumane.univaq.it/fileadmin/user_upload/ScienzeUmane/docenti/CV_DOCENTI_2025/Avolio_CV.pdf
  - 2018-19 syllabus: https://scienzeumane.univaq.it/fileadmin/user_upload/ScienzeUmane/PROGRAMMI_A.A.2018-2019/AVOLIO_DQ0048_2018-19.pdf
  - ALEICA atlas: https://scienzeumane.univaq.it/fileadmin/user_upload/ScienzeUmane/DIGITAL_HUMANITIES_PROGETTI/Atlante_Linguistico-Avolio.pdf
- **Confidence:** High on fit. Medium on route (the email is still to be found on the official page).
- **Caveat:** L'Aquila-area dialect is not the same as the Alto Sangro or Marsican variety. Ask him to flag whether "È ita a portà lu pane a mamma" and "Assù" are plausible for the Pescasseroli and Alto Sangro area specifically.

- **Route:** Corso Umberto I, 83, 65122 Pescara. Tel 085 4219109. Website: fondazionebrigatamaiella.it. A 2026 exhibition notice gives Corso Umberto I 87 for the Maison des Arts, which is the exhibition venue and not necessarily the office. No email was found.

## D. Search and rescue procedure

### 15. CNSAS, Soccorso Alpino e Speleologico Abruzzo (CNSAS-SASA)
- **Fit:** The exclusive reference body for mountain rescue in Abruzzo under L.R. 20/2014. It works under a convention with the Region and supports the 118 helicopter service. It is the right authority for how a missing-child search would be run: alert chain, incident command, dog units, use of the helicopter, what volunteers do, and the role of the Carabinieri and Prefettura.
- **Route:** The regional service page is on cnsas.it (the national site lists regional delegations). I could not find a regional email. A published interview names the regional president as Daniele Perilli (article in ildolomiti.it, 2025). Contact should go through the regional service or the Marsica station, not through him personally.
- **Sources:**
  - Regional convention (2026): https://trasparenza.regione.abruzzo.it/system/files/sovvenzioni/atti-concessione/8171/dpf027-05-2026-1.pdf
  - Radio network accord: https://www.montagna.tv/148501/rete-radio-montana-siglato-accordo-con-cnsas-abruzzo/amp/
- **Confidence:** High on fit, low on a direct email. National CNSAS publishes regional contacts, so check cnsas.it.
- **Question to put:** In the winter and summer cases, who coordinates a search for a child on park land (Prefettura, Carabinieri Forestali or the park guards), and when are CNSAS and dog units called in? Their answer would settle the plausibility of the search chapters.

## Suggested order of approach

1. **Dialect and customs:** Avolio (L'Aquila) first, then the Genti d'Abruzzo museum. Ask them to say whether the dialect phrases fit the Alto Sangro and Pescasseroli area, and whether the procession and the San Michele cave details are plausible. The Lettomanoppello and Pescocostanzo Sant'Angelo cults (pignora, incubatio, 8 May and 29 September feasts) are the closest Abruzzese parallels to compare against.
2. **History:** Fondazione Brigata Maiella and IASRIC for winter 1943-44. Note that Gustav Line events in the Alto Sangro (evacuation, requisition, German labour demands) are better known for the Sangro front, so ask about inland villages near the park.
3. **Bears and forest:** Park scientific or education office, plus Salviamo l'Orso and Rewilding Apennines.
4. **Search procedures:** CNSAS Abruzzo.

Before sending anything, include a one-page summary, the licence (CC BY 4.0) and a note that the text was written by an AI. A short list of specific questions per reviewer will get faster and better answers than the whole text.
